Corrections & updates log

A public record of corrections and significant updates to published issues of the Journal of the AI Universe, newest first.

How corrections are handled

Mistakes are corrected openly. When a correction is made, the item is amended and a dated Correction note is added to that issue’s page, saying what was changed and when; the issue page also shows the time of the latest update. Significant additions or changes that are not errors are listed as Updates. Factual errors are never changed silently. Typing and formatting fixes that do not change the meaning may be made without a note.

Times are given in Indian Standard Time (IST).

The full corrections policy is part of the editorial policies.

Report a correction

To report an error, post in the WhatsApp group, giving the date of the issue, the project number (01–50) and what is wrong, with a link to a source if you have one.
