Full write-up from 22 October 2026

1. Company and research division. Salesforce AI Research (S. Savarese, J. Emmons).

2. Date of publication / announcement. 19 October 2026 (Salesforce blog). Not covered in the previous edition; independent press discussion appeared on 21 October 2026.

3. Research title and field. Meet Launa-Omni, Salesforce's voice model for real-time conversation. Field: speech and spoken-dialogue models for enterprise agents.

4. Research background. Voice assistants are usually built either as a cascade (speech recognition, then a language model, then speech synthesis), which is easy to inspect but loses tone, pace and hesitation and adds delay, or as end-to-end speech-to-speech models, which sound natural but hide their reasoning and cost more to serve.

5. Scientific innovation. Launa-Omni takes a middle path. Its core is a text model retrofitted to take audio directly, so speech and language share one internal representation and cues such as emphasis are not discarded by a transcript. A separate in-house text-to-speech model voices the answer, and the text reply exists before it is spoken, so it can be inspected or corrected. A real-time harness detects when the speaker has finished. The model can be fine-tuned for names, dates, alphanumeric identifiers and industry terms.

6. Methodology. Evaluated on three public benchmarks: Audio MultiChallenge (multi-turn spoken dialogue and instruction retention; mean of three runs), VoiceBench (instruction following, reasoning and safety across nine datasets; same checkpoint) and τ³-Voice (voice customer service in retail, airline and telecom, run with a custom harness). Model size and training data were not disclosed beyond 'significantly smaller' than leading voice models.

7. Results.

  • Audio MultiChallenge: 59.69% (top public score shown: 60.40%).
  • VoiceBench: 90.60%. The public VoiceBench leaderboard lists this score from the authors' own submission.
  • τ³-Voice: 72.9% macro score (custom harness).
  • A simplified version (Steno) is in use in Slackbot Voice Dictation.

8. Scientific significance. An inspectable 'text-before-speech' design is a sensible answer to accountability concerns for voice agents that act on business records, and the benchmark results suggest a smaller model can be competitive.

9. Technical limitations.

  • All scores are self-reported; the τ³-Voice harness is custom, which limits comparability.
  • Parameter count, latency figures and training data were not published.
  • No availability, API or pricing has been announced.

10. Technology readiness and evidence type. Validated prototype (simplified version in production dictation). Evidence: company blog (marketing claims with benchmark scores; not independently reproduced).

11. Competitive comparison. Compared with Mistral's Voxtral 3 stack (covered in the previous edition), which overlaps separate ASR, LLM and TTS stages, Launa-Omni merges recognition into the language model but keeps synthesis separate. Benchmarks differ, so direct comparison is not possible.

12. Future research (1–5 years) — FORECAST. FORECAST (1–5 years): hybrid 'audio-in, text-visible, speech-out' designs are likely to become common for regulated enterprise voice agents; shared, independently run voice-agent benchmarks are needed.
