19 AI Work & Employment

Daily AI News Updates on AI Work & Employment: project 19 of 50, in the People & Life Stages group (15–20).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

AI is reshaping hiring rather than simply erasing jobs. The IMF finds hiring demand in AI-exposed jobs across Asia is nearly 10% lower, and a 41-country study finds AI adopters hire more senior staff and fewer juniors.

Top updates

  • IMF: AI cuts hiring demand in exposed jobs across Asia, CNBC-TV18 · 18 Oct 2026: Analysing 40 million online job postings in 19 Asian economies, the IMF's Regional Economic Outlook finds hiring demand in AI-exposed occupations nearly 10% below less-exposed roles, though the decline has stabilised. About 6% of firms in Asia ask for AI skills.
  • AI adoption linked to more hiring, fewer junior roles, King's College London · 6 Oct 2026: A study of 1.25 billion job adverts covering 158 million positions in 41 countries finds firm-level AI adoption lifts recruitment by about 3.3%; senior hiring rises 6.7% while junior hiring falls 2.5%.
  • Ireland: entry-level hiring in AI-augmented jobs drops 16%, The Irish Times · 14 Oct 2026: IDA Ireland's Labour Market Pulse found entry-level hiring in AI-augmented occupations fell 16% in Q2 2026, even as Ireland ranks fourth globally for AI engineering talent.
  • Software developer job postings are rising again, CNBC · 11 Oct 2026: US developer postings have climbed since February 2025, but 71% of the increase came from senior roles and 37% from AI-titled jobs; postings remain about 27.5% below pre-pandemic levels.

Research

  • Generative AI: first labour-market changes are affecting young people, Banque de France · 8 Oct 2026: In France, permanent hires of 21–29-year-olds in the most AI-exposed occupations are about 28% below their November 2022 level, against 8% for the least exposed.

Upcoming events

  • Reshaping Work 2026 – The Human Frontier, 12–13 Nov 2026, Amsterdam: Academics, policymakers, employers and workers' representatives on AI, work and social cohesion.
  • 26GHR – Global HR Conference, 11–13 Nov 2026, Tokyo: An international HR conference on talent, skills and AI-era work.

Why it matters

The first rung of the career ladder is where AI's labour impact shows first, so training, apprenticeships and graduate pathways matter more than ever.
