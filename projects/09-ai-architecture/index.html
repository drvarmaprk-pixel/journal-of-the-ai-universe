09 AI Architecture

Latest updates · 20 October 2026

In the full issue →

Buildings are getting world models: NanoTech's Roovie lets AI agents test design and retrofit decisions against real physics and utility bills, and Pirros opens firm design knowledge to AI assistants.

Research, industry & policy

  • NanoTech launches Roovie, a world model for buildings, Business Wire · 19 Oct 2026: Roovie gives each building a physics-based model where agents test design, operations and capital choices against cost, energy and comfort goals before money is spent; the meter then shows whether they were right.
  • Pirros brings firm design knowledge into AI assistants, ENGtechnica · 16 Oct 2026: A new MCP connection lets architecture and engineering teams search details and Revit families through ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor and Codex.

Conferences, workshops & events

  • 21st Advanced Building Skins Conference & Expo, 3–4 Nov 2026 · Bern: Keynote by Neil Leach on AI and the future of architectural design.
  • Journées thématiques IA pour l'architecture, 3–4 Nov 2026 · Grenoble: Research study days on AI for architecture.
  • AI Interiors Forum at interihotel MAD26, 17–19 Nov 2026 · Madrid (IFEMA): AI in everyday practice at architecture and interior-design studios.

Why it matters

Simulation-backed agents could make early design and retrofit decisions measurable, which matters for energy codes and payback.
