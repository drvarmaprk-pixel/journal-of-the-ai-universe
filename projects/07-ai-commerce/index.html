07 AI Commerce

Daily AI News Updates on AI Commerce: project 07 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

Shopping is becoming agentic. Shopify has opened its catalogue to more AI agents, Google has launched a cross-store Universal Cart in the US, and PayPal and Stripe are building checkout into AI experiences.

Top updates

  • Shopify opens its catalogue to more AI agents, The Verge · 13 Oct 2026: More AI agents can now reach Shopify's product catalogue, with Shop Pay checkout and buyer approval built in.
  • Google Universal Cart launches, Google · 15 Oct 2026: Now live in the US, it offers a cart that works across stores, price alerts and Google Pay checkout.
  • Glance and PayPal AI shopping, Business Wire · 15 Oct 2026: The partners are bringing AI-powered shopping and checkout to tens of millions of US consumers.
  • Constructor Agentic Checkout, PR Newswire · 5 Oct 2026: Powered by Stripe, it lets shoppers go from product discovery to purchase within a single AI-agent experience.

Research

  • StoreBench, arXiv · 7 Oct 2026: A live-commerce environment for evaluating and training autonomous agents that operate stores.
  • Get In, Decide, Prove, arXiv · 12 Oct 2026: A benchmark of how ready merchants are for agentic commerce.

Upcoming events

  • Retail Agent Con 2026, 17 Nov 2026, virtual: A conference on AI agents in retail, run by IHL Group.
  • Syndigo Connect Europe 2026, 18–19 Nov 2026, Zandvoort, Netherlands: Covers AI, product data and agentic commerce.
  • Tech for Retail 2026, from 30 Nov 2026, Paris: A retail-technology show.

Why it matters

When AI agents can find, compare and pay, brands must make their product data and checkout readable by agents, and keep humans approving purchases.
