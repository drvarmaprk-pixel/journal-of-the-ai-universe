Online stores are being rebuilt for AI shoppers: Shopify and Vercel have released tools for agent-ready storefronts, and Mastercard has opened early access to Agent Pay for Machines so AI agents can pay for digital services within set budgets.

Research, industry & policy

  • Shopify and Vercel arm stores for AI agents, Practical Ecommerce · 20 Oct 2026: Shopify's rebuilt Hydrogen SDK and Vercel Shop, an AI-enabled open-source storefront template, were presented at Vercel's Ship 26 conference as tools for stores serving humans and AI agents alike.New todayShopifyAI agentsOpen-source and open-weight AI
  • Mastercard opens early access to Agent Pay for Machines, Mastercard · 20 Oct 2026: Built on the open x402 protocol, agents sign payment vouchers within an approved limit; a Cloudflare Connect demo with agency Hype made nine payments to two merchants under one budget, after pilots with Cloudflare, Ant International, Coinbase, BVNK and others.New today
