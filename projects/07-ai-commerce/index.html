07 AI Commerce

Daily AI News Updates on AI Commerce: project 07 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 19 October 2026

In the full issue →

Agentic shopping is getting consent and payment plumbing. Shopify added buyer consent to its agent checkout tools, while retailers, banks and marketplaces debate how far to let AI agents in.

Research, industry & policy

  • Shopify: Checkout and Cart MCP support UCP buyer consent, Shopify developer changelog · 16 Oct 2026: Agents can now present each shop's privacy and marketing consent options during cart and checkout. Shop defaults by country apply when no consent is sent.
  • AI agents force retailers to rethink access and payments, PYMNTS · 16 Oct 2026: Retailers are reconsidering how they attract customers and authorise payments when AI agents do the shopping.
  • Amazon, eBay and banks shut out shopping agents, Ynet News · 18 Oct 2026: Security concerns, commercial interests and wary consumers are shaping autonomous shopping, from Amazon's case against Perplexity to banks blocking account access.

Journals & preprints

  • GLOBE: agentic memory for product listings, arXiv 2610.23709 · 16 Oct 2026: Structured, evolving memory helps agents optimise e-commerce product listings for generative search.

Conferences, workshops & events

  • AI in Retail, 16 Nov 2026 · London: Retail AI conference.
  • Agentiq World Amsterdam, 25–26 Nov 2026 · Amsterdam: Agentic commerce and AI agents.

Why it matters

Whoever controls consent and payment rails will shape how much of retail runs through AI agents.
