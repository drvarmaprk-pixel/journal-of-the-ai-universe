Does AI help students learn? A University of Maryland study links course AI assistants to lower grades and engagement, while OpenAI and Google push question-led study and free teacher training.

Top updates

  • UMD: AI course assistants linked to lower grades, University of Maryland · 16 Oct 2026: Course-integrated AI assistants were linked to lower grades and engagement, especially for first-generation students, which suggests more guidance is needed.
  • OpenAI 'AI for Educators' in Brazilian Portuguese, OpenAI · 15 Oct 2026: OpenAI launched the free course in Brazilian Portuguese and is promoting Study Mode for question-led learning.
  • Google expands AI Educator Series in India, Education Times · 15 Oct 2026: The free programme has trained more than 80,000 teachers since May and is adding new state partnerships.
  • University of Maine System signs ChatGPT Edu, Maine Public · 11 Oct 2026: The contract is intended to make AI use safer and cheaper.

Research

  • Verified, not generated, arXiv · 5 Oct 2026: Examines how expert-verified AI study materials spread learning gains across students in a university course.
  • Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI, arXiv · 9 Jul 2026 (older): Experimental evidence on how generative AI affects learning.

Upcoming events

  • AI for Education Conference 2026, 16–18 Nov 2026, NIE / NTU Singapore: A conference on AI in education.
  • Artificial Intelligence in Education Conference, 1–2 Dec 2026, virtual: Run by The Teaching Professor.

Why it matters

AI should make students think harder, not less. Design, guidance and verified materials decide whether it helps or hurts.
