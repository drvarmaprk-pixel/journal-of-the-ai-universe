AI education is being built into degrees and classrooms: IIT (BHU) Varanasi is creating a School of Physical AI and Autonomous Systems with a BTech from 2027, and an Illinois high school now lets students use AI when teachers allow it.

Research, industry & policy

  • IIT BHU to launch a School of Physical AI and Autonomous Systems, Education Times · 20 Oct 2026: The school will combine intelligence with sensors and machines, and a four-year, project-based BTech programme is reported to start in 2027.
  • New ETHS guidelines allow AI use in class at teachers' discretion, The Daily Northwestern · 20 Oct 2026: At Evanston Township High School, students may use AI for coursework if the teacher authorises it and they attach a disclosure statement explaining how they used it.
