Daily AI News Updates on AI Sports & Fitness: project 20 of 50, in the People & Life Stages group (15–20).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

AI is becoming both the athlete's coach and the fan's concierge. Suunto launched a conversational AI Coach, Tottenham Hotspur rolled out its multilingual Ask Spurs agent, and GNK ASG unveiled AI athlete tracking.

Top updates

  • Why we built Suunto AI Coach, Suunto · Oct 2026: Launched on 15 Oct with the Suunto Race 3S, the coach answers questions from a user's own training, sleep and recovery data and suggests workouts; three months are included with the watch.
  • Tottenham Hotspur launches Ask Spurs on Agentforce, Salesforce · 15 Oct 2026: The club's AI agent serves fans in 11 languages, with 86% engagement and 20% of conversations resolved by AI alone, across 4.6 million unified fan accounts.
  • GNK ASG unveils SPT sports performance tracking, GNK ASG · 12 Oct 2026: GPS, ultra-wideband and AI combine to estimate training load, readiness and injury risk in real time, using 20 Hz GNSS, ±10 cm indoor positioning and 64 parameters.
  • AI orchestration and sports fan engagement, Comcast Technology Solutions · 14 Oct 2026: LiveLike's CEO argues AI's biggest impact on fandom is orchestrating content, sponsorship and community moments in real time.

Research

  • TTNet: smart-racket table-tennis analysis, arXiv · 6 Oct 2026: A multitask deep-learning model reads smart-racket sensor data to analyse table-tennis strokes and player skill.

Upcoming events

  • SportsHCI 2026, 16–18 Nov 2026, Saarbrücken, Germany: ACM conference on technology, interaction and sport.
  • LEVEL UP Sports Tech Innovation Expo, 27–29 Nov 2026, Dubai Harbour, UAE: A sports-technology exhibition and conference.

Why it matters

AI turns wearable data into daily coaching decisions and helps clubs serve millions of fans — but injury and health calls should stay with qualified coaches and clinicians.
