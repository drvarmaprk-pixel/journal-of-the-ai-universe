25 AI Languages & Translation

Daily AI News Updates on AI Languages & Translation: project 25 of 50, in the Culture & Belief group (21–29).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

Translation AI is going open and real-time. Bilibili released open Index-Translate models covering 150 languages, Samsung widened Galaxy AI Live Translate to 24 languages, and Webex brought live speech translation to phone calls.

Top updates

  • Index-Translate open translation models, Bilibili · Oct 2026: An open family of 2B, 9B and 35B-A3B models for 150 languages, released on 30 Sep; a free API followed on 4 Oct and multimodal image translation on 10 Oct.
  • Samsung expands Galaxy AI language support, Samsung Newsroom · 14 Oct 2026: Hungarian and Malay take Live Translate and other Galaxy AI features to 24 languages; Bixby adds Polish, Thai and Vietnamese.
  • Translator Agent brings real-time translation to Webex Calling, Webex · 5 Oct 2026: Two-way speech translation inside calls is in controlled availability now, with general availability in November and Webex Meetings in early 2027.
  • Open Portuguese–Nheengatu translation models released, Oossa · 14 Oct 2026: Open models and a 14,500-sentence corpus for the Amazonian language Nheengatu; the best system scores 31.7–32.8 chrF++ on constitutional text.

Research

  • Back-Translation Still Matters, arXiv · 13 Oct 2026: For low-resource languages such as Odia and Swahili, back-translation still beats continued pre-training when parallel data is scarce.

Upcoming events

  • TC47 – Translating and the Computer, 8–10 Dec 2026, Luxembourg: AsLing's conference on translation technology and AI.
  • ALTA 2026, 30 Nov–2 Dec 2026, Melbourne: The Australasian Language Technology Association workshop.

Why it matters

Open, low-cost translation widens access to services, education and justice for speakers of smaller languages, not just the global majors.
