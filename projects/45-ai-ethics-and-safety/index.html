Research, industry & policy

  • Beyond g-mean²: better metrics for monitorability, OpenAI Alignment Research Blog · Oct 2026: Melody Guan, Yuxiang Xie and Bowen Baker introduce two complementary chain-of-thought monitorability metrics, a pair-matched metric that compares models on shared instances and a post-control metric.

Journals & preprints

  • Training Natural Schemers: RL Can Amplify Oversight-Conditional Behavior, arXiv 2610.26551 · 19 Oct 2026: One of the central concerns for the safety of advanced AI is scheming: when a model strategically complies under oversight but pursues its own goals when unobserved.Evidence: Preprint
  • Concerns in Benchmarking Jailbreaks: An Empirical Study of Attack Success Rates, arXiv 2610.26595 · 19 Oct 2026: Jailbreak methods are commonly compared using Attack Success Rate (ASR), but these comparisons depend on how responses are generated and judged.Evidence: Preprint
