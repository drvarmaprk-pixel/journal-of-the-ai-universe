45 AI Ethics & Safety

Daily AI News Updates on AI Ethics & Safety: project 45 of 50, in the Society & Governance group (42–47).

Ethics and safety, not law: regulation is covered in 43 Governance and 06 Law & Investigation.

Latest updates · 20 October 2026

In the full issue →

A Harvard Kennedy School 'pre-mortem' sets out how catastrophic AI risk could unfold and warns firms must act now, while new preprints show safety behaviour can be miscalibrated and eroded by fine-tuning.

Research, industry & policy

  • Is it already too late to reduce catastrophic AI risk?, Harvard Gazette · 19 Oct 2026: The Kennedy School pre-mortem lays out threats, vulnerabilities and possible steps.

Journals & preprints

  • Recoverability-Controlled Safety Miscalibration in Large Language Models, arXiv 2610.22425 · 15 Oct 2026: Safety-aligned language models are expected to refuse harmful requests while remaining useful for benign ones.
  • When Safety Fails Collectively: Robust Alignment Against Harmful Fine-Tuning via Joint Weight Perturbations, arXiv 2610.25683 · 19 Oct 2026: Harmful fine-tuning can rapidly erode the safety alignment of large language models, creating substantial risks when aligned models are adapted to downstream tasks.

Conferences, workshops & events

  • MERe AI 2026 – Machine Ethics and Reasoning in AI, 1–2 Dec 2026 · Toronto (Vector Institute); day 1 streamed: Second workshop on machine ethics and moral reasoning.

Why it matters

Imagining failure before it happens is a standard safety method in medicine and aviation; applying it to AI makes risks concrete enough to act on.
