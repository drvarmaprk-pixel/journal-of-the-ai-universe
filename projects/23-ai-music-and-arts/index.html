Research, industry & policy

  • 90,000 synthetic tracks a day: Deezer's AI 'kill switch', Music Business Worldwide · 19 Oct 2026: On peak days fully AI-generated tracks are more than half of new deliveries; an Ipsos survey found 45% of streaming users want to filter out 100% AI music.
  • YouTube Music begins labelling AI-generated songs, The Latent Daily · 19 Oct 2026: Uploaders can self-disclose, and SynthID or C2PA metadata can flag other tracks made or heavily altered with generative AI.

Journals & preprints

  • MUSEBENCH: A Benchmark for AI-Generated Music Detection and Artifact Analysis, arXiv 2610.20310 · 14 Oct 2026: Advances in generative AI have made it possible to produce music that is nearly indistinguishable from human compositions, raising the question of whether such works can be reliably distinguished from human compositions.
  • FLOMSS: Generative Music Source Separation by Flow Matching, arXiv 2610.17821 · 13 Oct 2026: Music source separation (MSS) has been conventionally addressed by discriminative methods.
