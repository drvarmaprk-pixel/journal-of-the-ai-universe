Music is drawing new lines on AI: Spotify has licensed fan-made AI remixes and covers from Believe and TuneCore artists who opt in, while the largest US musicians' union is demanding 'Human Made, Human Paid' protections.

Research, industry & policy

  • Believe and Spotify sign licensing deal for AI fan remixes and covers, Spotify · 20 Oct 2026: Participation in the upcoming tool is voluntary, with a two-step consent process: artists first opt in to AI programmes, and the Spotify partnership is then offered to those who opted in.
  • Largest US musicians' union demands AI protections, Rolling Stone · 20 Oct 2026: The American Federation of Musicians, which covers more than 80,000 professional artists, is asking major labels for contract safeguards under the banner 'Human Made, Human Paid'.

Journals & preprints
