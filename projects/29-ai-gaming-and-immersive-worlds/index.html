Research, industry & policy

  • Ludo.ai launches the Ludo CLI, GamesBeat · 19 Oct 2026: Developers can create production-ready game assets from the command line.
  • Polyscape and LivAI partner on AI tools, iHeartGeek · 19 Oct 2026: The partnership covers AI-powered QA, balance checks, IP supervision and compliance support.
  • ReVamp playtest limited to Discord over AI copying, The Verge · 19 Oct 2026: Moonlighter studio Digital Sun won't run a public Steam playtest because of AI-led cloning.

Journals & preprints

  • ROAR: Benchmarking LMM Agents under Real-Time Constraints in Multiplayer Action-Shooter Games, arXiv 2610.24282 · 16 Oct 2026: Large multimodal models (LMMs) are increasingly deployed as agents that perceive, reason, and act in interactive multi-agent environments.
