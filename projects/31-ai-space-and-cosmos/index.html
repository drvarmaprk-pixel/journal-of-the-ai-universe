31 AI Space & Cosmos

Daily AI News Updates on AI Space & Cosmos: project 31 of 50, in the Science group (30–35).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

AI is going into orbit and mapping the sky. China launched 10 satellites to test AI computing in space, an amateur used AI to find a possible hidden exoplanet, and Claude helped produce the first complete ultraviolet sky map.

Top updates

  • China launches satellites for space-based AI computing, Anadolu Agency · 15 Oct 2026: Ten satellites went up on a Zhuque-2E rocket to advance AI computing in space.
  • Amateur astronomer and AI find a possible exoplanet, Scientific American · 14 Oct 2026: An amateur astronomer used AI to spot a possible hidden exoplanet.
  • The missing map of the sky, Anthropic · 8 Oct 2026: Anthropic used Claude Science to produce the first complete map of the sky in ultraviolet light.
  • ESA's Earth Virtual Expert (EVE), ESA · Oct 2026: An open AI assistant for Earth-observation science, built with Mistral and Pi School.
  • NASA–IBM open lunar foundation model, The Decoder · 4 Oct 2026: The open-source model turns 17 years of Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) data into a foundation for lunar science.

Research

  • Graph attention network for galaxies and dark matter haloes, arXiv · 6 Oct 2026: Uses a graph attention network to predict galaxy and dark-matter-halo properties at the same time in GAMA DR4.
  • Space AI Infrastructure, arXiv · 12 Oct 2026: Examines where edge computing meets an 'orbital cloud' for AI in space.

Upcoming events

  • ESA Datalabs Euclid Hackathon 2026, 23–26 Nov 2026: Takes place after Euclid's first public data release on 12 Nov.
  • KIPAC Gaia DR4 × Agentic AI Sprint, 2–4 Dec 2026, Stanford: A sprint to decode the Milky Way using agentic AI on Gaia DR4.

Why it matters

Open models, open assistants and new data releases are turning space science into a field where professionals and amateurs alike can make discoveries.
