Latest updates · 18 October 2026

Kling 4.0 is the headline: Kuaishou's new model makes 30-second shots in a single pass with real directorial control. Hollywood and Korea are both moving AI into everyday production.

Top updates

  • Kling 4.0 officially launches, Kuaishou IR · 16 Oct 2026: Kuaishou launched Kling 4.0 on 15 Oct with native 30-second single-pass video, up to 15 reference assets and 10 keyframes, and 10-bit HDR. Support for 4K and 21:9 is still to come.
  • Culver City becomes an AI studio hub, Los Angeles Times · 16 Oct 2026: While Hollywood argues over AI, a cluster of AI filmmaking studios is forming in Culver City.
  • Korean directors bet on AI filmmaking, Korea JoongAng Daily · 16 Oct 2026: Korean directors and producers are committing to AI filmmaking now that video models have reached commercial quality.
  • Hollywood openly embraces AI, Variety · 13 Oct 2026: Studios are now using AI tools openly. Producer Ryan Kavanaugh says AI cut the budget of Doug Liman's Bitcoin from $200m to $70m.

Research

  • Any Time, Any Space, arXiv · 14 Oct 2026: A world-state-centric framework for agentic video generation. It keeps a persistent memory of scenes and events so multi-shot films stay consistent.
  • REEL, arXiv · 12 Oct 2026: Learns to retrieve, evaluate and evolve visual memory for long-horizon, multi-shot video generation.

Upcoming events

  • AIMA – AI Movie Awards, 7–8 Nov 2026, London: An awards event for AI-made films.
  • Tel Aviv AI Film Festival, 10 Nov 2026, Tel Aviv: A festival showcasing AI-generated cinema.
  • AI Toronto Film Awards, 13 Nov 2026, Toronto: Awards for AI filmmaking.

Why it matters

Longer, more controllable shots and lower budgets are moving AI from experiment to production. Directing skill and consistency between shots are becoming the edge.
