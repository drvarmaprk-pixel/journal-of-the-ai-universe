32 AI Biotechnology

Daily AI News Updates on AI Biotechnology: project 32 of 50, in the Science group (30–35).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

AI is starting to run the lab bench. Google's grounded AI agents ran closed-loop wet-lab cycles that found single-digit-nanomolar cancer-target binders, and Xaira went from start to antibody lead in 7 weeks.

Top updates

  • Grounded AI agents for autonomous wet labs, Google Research · Oct 2026: Grounded AI agents ran closed-loop wet-lab cycles and discovered single-digit-nanomolar binders to cancer targets.
  • Generate:Biomedicines × Zealand Pharma, Generate:Biomedicines · 13 Oct 2026: A collaboration and licence agreement to develop AI-engineered protein therapeutics for metabolic disease.
  • Receptor.AI opens its peptide platform, Receptor.AI · 14 Oct 2026: The company has opened access to its end-to-end AI platform for peptide discovery.
  • Xaira de novo antibodies, Xaira Therapeutics · 2 Oct 2026: Xaira reports de novo AI-designed, developable antibodies, with 7 weeks from start to lead molecule.
  • Hansa Biopharma partners with Cradle, Hansa Biopharma · 1 Oct 2026: A partnership for AI-driven protein design and engineering.

Research

  • Multi-objective amortised CDRH3 sampling, arXiv · 12 Oct 2026: Samples CDRH3 sequences against several objectives at once to diversify promising antibody candidates.
  • Blinded, prospective benchmark of in-silico antibody discovery, Nature Biotechnology · 19 Aug 2026 (older): A benchmark anchored to experimentally measured affinity and developability.

Upcoming events

  • AI/ML for Drug Discovery, Nov 2026, Barcelona: Run by Cambridge Healthtech Institute. Editor's note: the event page lists both 10–11 and 11–12 Nov, so confirm the dates first.
  • 28th BioPharma Drug Discovery Nexus (West), 11–12 Nov 2026, San Diego: A drug-discovery conference.

Why it matters

Closed-loop AI design and testing is shrinking drug-discovery timelines from years to months.
