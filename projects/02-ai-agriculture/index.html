02 AI Agriculture

Daily AI News Updates on AI Agriculture: project 02 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 19 October 2026

In the full issue →

Indian crop science led the week. Google DeepMind and ICRISAT will build an open AI sorghum atlas, Google's farm models moved to public preview, and Korea released a free AI tool that times rice-seed harvests.

Research, industry & policy

  • Google DeepMind and ICRISAT plan an open sorghum genetic atlas, ICRISAT · 16 Oct 2026: Google DeepMind and Google.org will work with ICRISAT, HudsonAlpha and the University of Washington on an open atlas built from ICRISAT's genebank of 44,000+ samples from 90+ countries. It comes with a three-year DeepMind postdoctoral fellowship to extend the tools to pulses.
  • Agricultural Understanding models in public preview, Google India · 15 Oct 2026: The ALU and AMED APIs now cover 11 countries. In Gujarat, Amnex built them into the state's Krushi Pragati platform, which has sent 900M+ SMS advisories to 600,000+ farmers. The EkStep-led OpenAgriNet uses them in a dashboard for administrators.
  • AI predicts the best harvest time for rice seed, Korea.net · 15 Oct 2026: The Korea Seed and Variety Service and KETI built a free tool that uses 46 years of temperature data. A pilot on 9,565 plots of 24 varieties was accurate to within ±3 days.

Journals & preprints

  • A100: a vision foundation model for plant images, arXiv 2610.22991 · 16 Oct 2026: A hybrid vision foundation model for crop monitoring, disease diagnosis and phenotyping, designed for settings with little annotated data.
  • Potato leaf disease CNN tested on Ethiopian field data, arXiv 2610.20544 · 14 Oct 2026: A lightweight CNN for early and late blight, trained on 2,215 PlantVillage images and then tested on 247 independent field images.
  • MARTS: a targeted-spraying robot for tomatoes, arXiv 2610.18123 · 13 Oct 2026: A low-cost greenhouse robot that links plant-level disease detection to spraying of individual leaves.

Conferences, workshops & events

  • FAO Science and Innovation Forum 2026 (wrap-up), 12–16 Oct 2026 · Rome and online (FAO): Ended on 16 Oct. Sessions included AI-enabled digital agronomy and extension (14 Oct) and women farmers in the digital age (15 Oct).
  • ICAR-IASRI training: AI for genomic prediction in plant breeding, 22–28 Oct 2026 · ICAR-IASRI, New Delhi: An offline course (₹2,000; applications closed 28 Sep) covering GWAS, genomic prediction and machine learning for crop improvement.
  • Agriculture AI on WhatsApp (KissanAI with Meta), 27 Oct 2026 · Meta, One BKC, Mumbai: A free, invite-only morning session with Indic voice agents, crop-photo vision and a live WhatsApp workflow build.

Why it matters

Open genetic resources and public farm-data APIs let breeders and agritech start-ups build on shared foundations, especially for smallholders in drylands.
