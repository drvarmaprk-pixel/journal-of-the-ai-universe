02 AI Agriculture

Daily AI News Updates on AI Agriculture: project 02 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 20 October 2026

In the full issue →

A PNAS study finds smart farming already pays on 42% of the world's cropland, Karnataka is tendering an AI and satellite crop-monitoring pilot, and greenhouse AI is consolidating in Europe and Korea.

Research, industry & policy

  • Karnataka to use AI and satellite imagery to monitor crops (India), Moneycontrol · 19 Oct 2026: The Karnataka State Remote Sensing Applications Centre has called bids for a pilot using satellite imagery, drones and AI to map crops, detect pests and diseases and estimate village-level yields in drought-hit regions.
  • Source.ag acquires Optimal for autonomous greenhouse control, AgroSpectrum Asia · 19 Oct 2026: Amsterdam's Source.ag buys London start-up Optimal, which reported 13% higher yield and 27% lower energy use in a Dutch greenhouse. First autonomous deployments are planned for winter 2026–27 in Europe and North America.
  • Korea field-tests AI-TEMPI greenhouse climate control, HortiSignal · 19 Oct 2026: The Rural Development Administration is testing its AI temperature-control system at nine farms. In an internal trial, tomatoes reached 10.5 kg per plant, about 98% of an expert-managed greenhouse.

Journals & preprints

  • Modeling the global potential of smart farming innovations (PNAS), PNAS · DOI 10.1073/pnas.2530081123 · 19 Oct 2026: University of Bonn-led study: robots, drones and AI already pay off on 42% of cropland, rising to 50% in 20 years and up to 75% if all countries tightened environmental rules.
  • Multi-Speed Field Evaluation of a Semi-Autonomous Precision Sprayer for Weed Control in Vegetable Crops, arXiv 2610.24893 · 17 Oct 2026: Machine vision-guided precision spraying is promising for sustainable vegetable production by enabling site-specific weed targeting, reducing herbicide application while protecting crop yield and quality.
  • ADA 2025: A Multimodal Dataset for Water-Deficit Stress Detection in Sugar Beets, arXiv 2610.23609 · 16 Oct 2026: Research and development into artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) applications in crop management are hindered by a lack of suitable public datasets.

Conferences, workshops & events

  • ICA 2026 – AI Meets Farm, 1–4 Nov 2026 · Rupnagar, Punjab (IIT Ropar): Digital agriculture conference: workshops 1 Nov, keynotes and papers 2–4 Nov.
  • Digital Agriculture: Precision Application conference, 17–18 Nov 2026 · Cologne and live stream: 3rd international conference on regulatory and scientific aspects of precision application.
  • AgroTech 2026, 3–5 Dec 2026 · Lucknow (ICAR-IISR): 25th edition; themes include Digital Agriculture and AI Solutions, ICT, AI & Extension.

Why it matters

Profitability, not technology, decides adoption; the PNAS map shows where precision farming pays first, and state pilots like Karnataka's test it where water stress is worst.
