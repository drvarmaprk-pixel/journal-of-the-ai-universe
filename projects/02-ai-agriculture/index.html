AI is reaching the barn and the food lab: researchers screened millions of molecules to find potent cattle methane inhibitors, and Penn State built agents that pull food-science data from papers in minutes.

Research, industry & policy

  • Reservoir Farms opens a physical-AI farm site in Washington State, AgFunderNews · 20 Oct 2026: The robotics testbed will open in the Yakima Valley in spring 2027 for tree-fruit and blueberry trials, with more than 30 member start-ups and a $10 million, three-year pledge from John Deere.Evidence: Company announcement (evidence step 1 of 6)New todayJohn Deere
  • Penn State's AgentXtract mines food-science papers, Agri-Pulse · 21 Oct 2026: A multi-agent system checked by human experts pulled more than 4,000 experimental records in about 30 minutes, work the team says would take six months or more by hand, to support food quality and safety research.Evidence: Laboratory demonstration (evidence step 3 of 6)New todayAI agents

Journals & preprints

  • Discovery of potent inhibitors of rumen methane metabolism via an AI-assisted workflow, npj Biofilms and Microbiomes · 21 Oct 2026: Researchers screened about 23 million molecules, tested 26 and found 4 active; the best cut methane by 73–98% in rumen samples from four ruminant sources in the laboratory.Evidence: Peer-reviewed (evidence step 5 of 6)Key finding: An AI screen of about 23 million molecules gave four active compounds that cut rumen methane by 73–98% in laboratory tests.Significance: Technical: AI shortlisting turned a huge chemical space into a handful of lab-confirmed leads for livestock emissions.Takeaway: Promising lab results; animal trials, safety and effects on milk and growth are still needed.New today

Conferences, workshops & events

  • Asia-Pacific Agri-Food Innovation Summit, 27–29 Oct 2026 · Singapore: Agri-food technology meeting from the World Agri-Tech organisers.New today

Why it matters

Livestock methane and slow food-science data work are both real bottlenecks; these items show AI shortening the search, with lab and field tests still ahead.
