26 AI Fashion & Beauty

Daily AI News Updates on AI Fashion & Beauty: project 26 of 50, in the Culture & Belief group (21–29).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

Beauty and fashion are becoming AI-guided. Pinterest turns hair and nail Pins into step-by-step guides, Perfect Corp brings selfie skin diagnosis to LIERAC in eight European markets, and Gap adds conversational shopping.

Top updates

  • Pinterest launches Beauty Guides, Pinterest · 6 Oct 2026: AI turns hair and nail Pins into personalised guides — what to ask your stylist, similar looks, products and steps; on iOS in the US.
  • Perfect Corp and LIERAC bring AI skin diagnosis to Europe, Business Wire · 14 Oct 2026: A selfie gives a skin score across seven ageing concerns, with routines, in eight European markets; the model is trained on 70,000+ images.
  • Gap expands AI shopping with Alta Daily, Digital Commerce 360 · 7 Oct 2026: Natural-language product discovery arrives at Old Navy and Banana Republic, and Gap merchandise joins AI-generated outfits on Alta Daily.
  • Justmylook adds AI-readable ingredient data for 15 brands, EIN Presswire · 15 Oct 2026: Structured ingredient data helps AI assistants recommend products; it cites Adobe data that 42% of US consumers have used AI assistants to shop.

Research

  • ShoeFit: virtual footwear try-on, arXiv · 13 Oct 2026: A diffusion-based try-on method for shoes, with a new multi-view MVShoes dataset.

Upcoming events

  • Feeling AI 4 Beauty 2026, 18 Nov 2026, Barcelona: AI in cosmetics and beauty.
  • Fashion Reboot 2026, 19 Nov 2026, IFM Paris: Institut Français de la Mode's day on fashion, consumers, AI and innovation.

Why it matters

AI advice now shapes what people buy and put on their skin, so transparent data and realistic claims matter as much as clever try-ons.
