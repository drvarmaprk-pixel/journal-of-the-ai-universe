Latest updates · 19 October 2026

In the full issue →

Clinical AI got both governance and scale this week. FDA comments on generative-AI devices close today, India set out its AI tools for TB, and a Sydney children's network is preserving ICU data for future AI.

Research, industry & policy

  • FDA: regulating generative-AI medical devices, US FDA (CDRH) · comments close 19 Oct 2026: Discussion paper and request for feedback on generative-AI devices (docket FDA-2026-N-7874).
  • India's AI tools against TB, PIB (MoHFW) · 13 Oct 2026: 500+ AI handheld X-ray units are deployed, with 1,500 more coming. Cough Against TB has screened 1.62 lakh people with 12–16% extra yield, DeepCXR runs in 8 states/UTs, and there is a 'Khushi' chatbot.
  • Sydney Children's preserves ICU data for future AI, Healthcare IT News · 18 Oct 2026: 'Big Data for Small People' (with Philips) keeps paediatric ICU waveforms and alarms for future AI decision support.

Journals & preprints

  • Ambient AI scribes over 150 days in primary care, JAMIA · doi 10.1093/jamia/ocag151 · 2026 (advance article): By day 150, note time fell 15% and after-hours time fell 18% (WashU/BJC: 220 clinicians, 314,845 encounters; work RVUs up 2%).
  • MedPAO-VL: protocol-compliant radiology reports, arXiv 2610.21794 · 15 Oct 2026: An interpretable agent that writes structured radiology reports following reporting protocols, aiming to reduce ungrounded findings.
  • AI Consilium: benchmarking LLM second opinions, arXiv 2610.22298 · 15 Oct 2026: Scores second opinions on 300 NEJM cases for grounding, actionability and safety.

  • CSICON 2026 (cardiology, India), 3–6 Dec 2026 · Palace Grounds, Bengaluru: CSI's 78th annual conference, with an AI in Cardiology track.

  • ML4H 2026 Symposium, 6–7 Dec 2026 · Sydney: Mentorship applications close 26 Oct.
