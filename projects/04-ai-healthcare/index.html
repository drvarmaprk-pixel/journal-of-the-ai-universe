US regulators are shaping how generative AI medical devices will be judged: an FDA digital-health chief says they may need to be assessed more like physicians, and the FDA has posted worked examples of AI devices that update themselves under pre-approved plans.

Research, industry & policy

  • FDA official: GenAI tools may need physician-style assessment, MedCity News · 20 Oct 2026: At AdvaMed's MedTech Conference in Boston, Rick Abramson of FDA's Digital Health Center of Excellence said the agency received more than 300 comments on its GenAI device discussion paper and intends to draft policy.New todayUS FDA
  • FDA outlines AI device modification scenarios under PCCPs, FDAnews · 20 Oct 2026: Two hypothetical examples show a hospital-specific sepsis alert being fine-tuned on local data and a cardiac arrhythmia detector being retrained automatically, both within predetermined change control plans that avoid a new submission per change.New todayUS FDACardiology
