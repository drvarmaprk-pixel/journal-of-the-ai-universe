Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

The AMA has set rules for agentic AI in the clinic: authority must be 'earned through evidence'. On the same day the FDA cleared an MRI-based AI that estimates breast-cancer recurrence risk.

Top updates

  • AMA governance framework for agentic AI, Fierce Healthcare · 16 Oct 2026: The American Medical Association has released a framework for AI agents that take on clinical functions. Under it, an agent's authority is 'earned through evidence'.
  • FDA clears TumorSight Risk, Fierce Biotech · 16 Oct 2026: SimBioSys won FDA clearance for TumorSight Risk, an Nvidia-powered tool that uses MRI data to predict the risk of breast-cancer recurrence.
  • JAMA AI journal announced, Fierce Healthcare · 16 Oct 2026: JAMA Network will launch JAMA AI, a new online journal, in early 2027, with AI-assisted peer review.
  • Duke AI Health weekly roundup, Duke AI Health · 16 Oct 2026: Duke's weekly round-up of health-AI research and policy.

Research

  • An open vision-language model for diverse medical applications, Nature Medicine · 6 Oct 2026: An openly available vision-language model that works across a wide range of medical uses.

Upcoming events

  • RSNA 2026, 29 Nov–3 Dec 2026, McCormick Place, Chicago: The radiology meeting includes an AI Showcase with more than 100 AI exhibitors.
  • AHA Scientific Sessions 2026 – Health Innovation, 7–9 Nov 2026: Health-innovation programming covering AI agents and clinical AI.

Why it matters

Clear, evidence-based rules for clinical AI agents and FDA-cleared prediction tools help clinicians adopt AI safely.
