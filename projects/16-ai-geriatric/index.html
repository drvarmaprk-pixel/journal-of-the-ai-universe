Daily AI News Updates on AI Geriatric: project 16 of 50, in the People & Life Stages group (15–20).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

AI is quietly entering elder care. Duke has launched a national hub for trustworthy ageing and dementia AI, Korea plans round-the-clock AI eldercare from 2028, and Taiwan turns family photos into reminiscence videos. (A quieter category: the window was widened to about 14 days, 4–15 Oct.)

Top updates

  • Duke launches national hub for trustworthy ageing AI, Duke School of Medicine · 12 Oct 2026: The hub will advance trustworthy AI for ageing and dementia care.
  • AI in dementia care: help, not replacement, CU Anschutz · 15 Oct 2026: Geriatric and ethics experts say AI can help caregivers but cannot replace human connection.
  • South Korea's AI care roadmap, The Korea Herald · 6 Oct 2026: The roadmap plans companion robots, smart sensors and round-the-clock AI eldercare from 2028.
  • Taiwan's AI reminiscence videos, Focus Taiwan · 4 Oct 2026: Taiwan's Institute for Information Industry (III) turns old photos and voices into personalised AI reminiscence videos for people with dementia.

Research

  • A new fall detection system design, arXiv · 5 Oct 2026: A fall-detection system that combines landmark identification with deep learning.

Upcoming events

  • GSA 2026 Annual Scientific Meeting, 4–7 Nov 2026, National Harbor, Maryland: Includes an AgeTech track.
  • APDA 'AI for Parkinson's: From Data to Care in an Aging World', 19 Nov 2026: A session on AI in Parkinson's care.

Why it matters

The best eldercare technology supports carers and protects dignity. It does not replace human warmth.
