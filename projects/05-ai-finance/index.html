05 AI Finance

Daily AI News Updates on AI Finance: project 05 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 21 October 2026

In the full issue →

Banks are opening and closing doors to AI agents at the same time: Visa, BILL and Billtrust have a live pilot of agents paying supplier invoices, HSBC is letting clients' own AI query their data, and RBC may block Meta's Muse agent.

Research, industry & policy

  • Visa, BILL and Billtrust launch live pilot of AI agent invoice payments, Visa · 20 Oct 2026: Using Visa Trusted Agent Protocol, trusted AI agents complete approved buyer invoice payments end to end through supplier portals.
  • HSBC turns to 'embedded AI' with HSBCnio for business clients, American Banker · 20 Oct 2026: Through Model Context Protocol access, clients' own AI tools can query authorised account and transaction data.
  • RBC considers blocking Meta's Muse AI agent, Financial Post (Bloomberg) · 20 Oct 2026: CEO Dave McKay said no final decision has been made; JPMorgan and Charles Schwab are blocking or limiting outside AI agents' access to client data.

Why it matters

Agentic payments need both trust rails and clear limits; banks are drawing the line between agents they authenticate and agents they cannot monitor. News summary only, not financial advice.
