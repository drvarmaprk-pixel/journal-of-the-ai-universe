05 AI Finance

Daily AI News Updates on AI Finance: project 05 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

Regulators are setting guardrails while banks accelerate. The FSB has finalised 12 sound practices for responsible AI, and JPMorgan is keeping outside AI agents away from customer accounts.

Top updates

  • FSB finalises AI sound practices, Financial Stability Board · 15 Oct 2026: The FSB has finalised 12 sound practices for financial institutions adopting AI responsibly.
  • JPMorgan blocks outside AI agents, CNBC · 13 Oct 2026: JPMorgan is blocking third-party AI agents, such as Muse and Instinct, from accessing customer accounts.
  • Bank CEOs on AI at Q3 earnings, Banking Dive · 15 Oct 2026: Leaders of Citi, Wells Fargo, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, BofA and JPMorgan used Q3 earnings to discuss AI productivity and accountability.
  • MAS AI risk guidelines, Monetary Authority of Singapore · 7 Oct 2026: MAS has issued Guidelines on AI Risk Management, setting out its supervisory expectations for financial institutions.

Research

  • Knowledge or Calculator?, arXiv · 2 Oct 2026: Separates how much of an AI financial agent's performance comes from domain knowledge and how much from calculation, using workflows whose results can be checked.
  • Citation-grounded earnings-call benchmark, arXiv · 1 Oct 2026: A benchmark that tests whether LLM analysis of earnings-call transcripts is trustworthy and properly cited.

Upcoming events

  • ACM ICAIF 2026, 14–17 Nov 2026 (workshops 14–15 Nov), Bocconi University, Milan: The International Conference on AI in Finance.

Why it matters

With global and national rulebooks arriving, banks will need governance, audit trails and control over agent access as they scale AI.
