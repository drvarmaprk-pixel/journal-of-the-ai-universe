05 AI Finance

Daily AI News Updates on AI Finance: project 05 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 19 October 2026

In the full issue →

Banks are launching assistants while card networks tighten controls on agents. Mizuho rolled out an OpenAI-powered voice assistant, Backbase launched governed conversational banking, and Amex set out how to verify agent purchases.

Research, industry & policy

  • Mizuho launches the 'Aomaru Bank' AI assistant, Mizuho FG (PR Times) · 14 Oct 2026: The assistant answers from 1,700+ FAQs and cites its sources, with real-time voice built on OpenAI technology. At launch it does not access account data, and the data is not used to train OpenAI models.
  • Backbase launches Conversational Banking, Backbase · Oct 2026: AI agents complete banking requests by voice and text, within the permissions and approvals each bank defines.
  • Amex Intent Intelligence for agent purchases, Digital Commerce 360 · 16 Oct 2026: Records what a shopper authorised before an AI agent buys, to cut disputes, including over agent ordering mistakes.

Journals & preprints

  • Same person, different advice, arXiv 2610.23060 · 16 Oct 2026: Audits LLM financial advisers. In a US panel, 41% of households changed their risk-tolerance answer within two years, which shifts the advised allocation.
  • Where backtests and live trading part ways, arXiv 2610.21846 · 15 Oct 2026: Six defects found by comparing an intraday system's live days with backtest replays.

Conferences, workshops & events

  • Money20/20 USA (under way), 18–21 Oct 2026 · Las Vegas: The fintech flagship, with agentic payments high on the agenda.
  • Singapore FinTech Festival 2026, 18–20 Nov 2026 · Singapore (MAS): Explores the five forces rewiring global finance.

Why it matters

As agents start to act for customers, banks need traceable consent, permissions and audit trails before they can scale. News summary only, not financial advice.
