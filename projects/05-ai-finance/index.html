05 AI Finance

Daily AI News Updates on AI Finance: project 05 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 20 October 2026

In the full issue →

Banks are scaling AI and fencing it in at once: Santander halved costs in a customer-service test on NVIDIA infrastructure, Emirates NBD is running OpenAI models in the UAE, and Citi and Bank of America are limiting outside AI agents.

Research, industry & policy

  • Santander builds AI capabilities on NVIDIA infrastructure, Santander · 19 Oct 2026: In an experimental customer-service use case, Santander improved conversation quality while cutting costs by more than 50%, and set a three-year framework for the next phase with NVIDIA.
  • Emirates NBD runs OpenAI models in the UAE, Gulf News · 19 Oct 2026: Emirates NBD is the first MENA bank to deploy OpenAI models on UAE infrastructure, for customer engagement, compliance and productivity.
  • Citi and Bank of America restrict AI agent access, The Paypers · 19 Oct 2026: The US lenders are limiting third-party AI agents' access to customer accounts amid concerns over deposit outflows, payment liability and regulatory clarity.

Journals & preprints

  • From SEC Filings to Financial Signals: Evaluating LLMs on Stock Market Reactions and Analyst Forecasts, arXiv 2610.24268 · 16 Oct 2026: Form 10-Q MD&A sections combine long-form management narrative with accounting and forward-looking information.
  • ACE-Trader: Adversarial Co-Evolution for Generator-Critic Trading Systems, arXiv 2610.25847 · 19 Oct 2026: Generator--critic trading systems pair an LLM-based trader that makes trading decisions with a red team agent that evaluates these decisions and provides feedback for revision.

  • Building a Future-Ready Bank, 27 Nov 2026 · Mumbai: Banking leaders on AI-driven decisioning and core modernisation.

Why it matters

Banks want AI inside and are wary of AI outside: in-house models cut costs, while third-party agents raise questions of liability and customer ownership. News summary only, not financial advice.
