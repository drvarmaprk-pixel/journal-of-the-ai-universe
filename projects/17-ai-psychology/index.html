17 AI Psychology

Daily AI News Updates on AI Psychology: project 17 of 50, in the People & Life Stages group (15–20).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

New research shows chatbots often fail people at the moment of crisis, and regulators are responding. JAMA Psychiatry calls for clearer rules, and US states are restricting AI 'therapy' that lacks licensed oversight.

Top updates

  • JAMA Psychiatry: regulate mental-health AI agents, JAMA Network · 14 Oct 2026: A Viewpoint calls for clearer regulation of AI agents used in mental health.
  • US state laws on AI therapy, Baker Botts · 14 Oct 2026: A wave of state laws, in Illinois, Nevada, Colorado and elsewhere, now restricts AI therapy without licensed oversight.
  • RAND: chatbot suicide safety varies, Telehealth.org · 14 Oct 2026: A RAND study in JMIR found that chatbot answers to suicide-related questions differ between developer APIs and consumer apps.
  • Chatbots fail to route crises to humans, TIME · 9 Oct 2026: New research finds AI chatbots often fail to direct users in a mental-health crisis to human help.

Research

  • What do chatbots do after users decline therapeutic redirection?, arXiv · 15 Oct 2026: Examines chatbot behaviour after a user with suicidal thoughts declines a suggestion to seek therapy.
  • DistressBench, arXiv · 8 Oct 2026: A benchmark that evaluates crisis support from large language models across several dimensions.

Upcoming events

  • PAWNY Fall 2026 Workshop: Ethical Challenges in Mental Health Practice Presented by Technology, 7 Nov 2026, Zoom: Continuing-education (CE) credits are available.
  • International Congress on AI & Digital Psychiatry 2026, 25–26 Nov 2026, online: A congress on AI and digital psychiatry.

Why it matters

Crisis handling is where mental-health AI must be safest, and evidence and law are now converging on licensed human oversight. If you are struggling, please reach out to a real person or a local helpline.
