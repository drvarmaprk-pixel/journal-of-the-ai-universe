New evidence tests chatbot safety in crisis conversations. A controlled study found that how context is presented changes whether models intervene in time.

Research, industry & policy

  • How LLMs respond to escalating suicide risk, Frontiers in Psychiatry · doi 10.3389/fpsyt.2026.1962098 · 16 Oct 2026: Researchers tested ChatGPT, Gemini and Doubao across 12 five-turn scenarios. Timely intervention reached 87.5% when the user's history was gathered into one prompt, against 50% in native multi-turn chat and 41.7% for single turns. Results varied by model.
