AI is sharpening weather and climate forecasts: AI models gave some of the most accurate track predictions for Hurricane Isaias, and New Zealand researchers produced high-resolution climate projections in six hours instead of years.

Research, industry & policy

  • AI models are transforming hurricane forecasting, WBUR Here & Now (WUSF) · 20 Oct 2026: AI models provided some of the most accurate predictions of the track of Hurricane Isaias.New today
  • Generative AI unlocks extreme-weather insights for New Zealand, Earth Sciences New Zealand · 20 Oct 2026: The AI produced more than 15,000 simulated years of daily rainfall and maximum temperature at 12 km resolution through to 2100, within six hours, a task that would have taken years conventionally.New today

Journals & preprints

  • AirMPA: A Meteorology-to-Pollution Adapter for Global Air Quality Forecasting, arXiv 2610.25797 · 19 Oct 2026: Accurate real-time forecasting of atmospheric pollutants is essential for reducing exposure risks and improving public health.Evidence: Preprint (evidence step 4 of 6)New today
  • Zephyrus-R1: Agentic Reinforcement Learning for Weather Reporting, arXiv 2610.25625 · 19 Oct 2026: Reinforcement learning (RL) has emerged as a key method for training AI models in scientific applications, but RL remains difficult when outputs cannot easily be verified against a known ground-truth answer.Evidence: Preprint (evidence step 4 of 6)New todayAI agentsReinforcement learning

Why it matters
