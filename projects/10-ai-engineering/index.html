Agents are learning to drive engineering software: Neural Concept's MCP Bridge lets them run CATIA, NX and Abaqus inside enterprise systems, and Flexcompute's PhotonForge 2.0 shares photonic-chip design with AI agents.

Research, industry & policy

  • Neural Concept launches MCP Bridge for CAD and simulation, SiliconANGLE · 19 Oct 2026: AI agents can now operate CATIA, NX, ANSA, HyperMesh and Abaqus inside enterprise environments, from CAD to mesh to solver.
  • Flexcompute releases PhotonForge 2.0, ENGtechnica · 19 Oct 2026: Engineers and AI agents share one photonic-chip workflow from schematic and layout through simulation, verification and tapeout; the Community edition is free for research and commercial use.

Journals & preprints

  • IndustrialCAD-1400: Benchmarking Detailed Industrial Assemblies from Text, arXiv 2610.24789 · 17 Oct 2026: Industrial assemblies require more than a recognizable shape: functional parts, interfaces, dimensions and service features must satisfy a detailed request together.
  • Assessing the CAD Capabilities of General-Purpose AI Models: A Case Study of GPT-6 Astra, arXiv 2610.24451 · 17 Oct 2026: Recent advances in general-purpose AI models and agentic systems have enabled them to perform some CAD tasks.

Conferences, workshops & events

  • IEEE INDICON 2026, 18–20 Dec 2026 · Chennai (Sri Sairam Engineering College): Theme: Net-Zero Cyber-Physical Intelligence – AI, 6G & sustainable electronics.
