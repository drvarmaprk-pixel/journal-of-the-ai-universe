AI agents are learning to drive engineering software directly: Neural Concept's MCP Bridge lets agents operate CAD and simulation tools such as CATIA, NX and Abaqus, and WSCAD has unveiled an 'engineering intelligence' platform for electrical design.

Research, industry & policy

  • Neural Concept launches MCP Bridge for CAD and simulation agents, Neural Concept · 19 Oct 2026: AI agents can now operate commercial tools such as CATIA, NX and Abaqus inside enterprise environments, with each workflow step routed to the model that benchmarked best on that engineering task.
  • WSCAD unveils ELECTRIX OS, WSCAD via Business Wire · 20 Oct 2026: The electrical CAD maker presents ELECTRIX OS as a new category of 'engineering intelligence' software for electrical and automation engineering.

Journals & preprints

  • Can It Actually Work? Synthesizing Functional CAD Mechanisms, arXiv 2610.26861 · 19 Oct 2026: While LLM agents can generate visually plausible CAD assemblies, these designs often fail to fulfill their intended physical functions.

Conferences, workshops & events

  • 3rd Indo-Japan Workshop on AI in CFD, 28–29 Nov 2026 · Chennai (IIT Madras): Registration open until 25 Nov.
