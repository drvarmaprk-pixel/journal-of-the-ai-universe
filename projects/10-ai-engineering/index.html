Daily AI News Updates on AI Engineering: project 10 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

Engineering software is going agentic. Cadence, Altium and Autodesk all shipped AI agents this week, Nvidia released an open geometry kernel for AI-ready CAD, and Siemens' PhysicsAI predicts simulations in seconds.

Top updates

  • Nvidia open USD BRep geometry kernel, DEVELOP3D · 13 Oct 2026: An open geometry kernel for solid modelling, aimed at CAD workflows that work well with AI.
  • Cadence Virtuoso AI Studio IC26.1, Cadence · 14 Oct 2026: The release adds the 'ViraStack AI Super Agent' for analogue and custom design flows.
  • Altium Agentic Requirements Engineering, Altium · 14 Oct 2026: A new feature in Requirements Portal in which a human approves every AI action.
  • Autodesk AI-native APS workflow, Autodesk · 14 Oct 2026: Autodesk previews APS Workflow MCP, an AI-native workflow for Autodesk Platform Services developers.
  • Siemens Simcenter PhysicsAI, Siemens · 8 Oct 2026: Engineers can train it on existing structural and fluid simulation data so it predicts 3D fields in seconds.

Research

  • Rethinking Human Intent-to-CAD, arXiv · 11 Oct 2026: Generates parametric CAD models from human intent, using cooperative multi-task alignment and spatially aware reinforcement learning.
  • DeciCAD, arXiv · 15 Oct 2026: Teaches CAD agents action policies through learning grounded in a test harness.

Upcoming events

  • COMSOL Day: AI for Modeling and Simulation, 19 Nov 2026: A day on AI for modelling and simulation.
  • ESIC UAE 2026, 19 Nov 2026, Dubai: Covers AI-enhanced simulation, multiphysics and digital twins.

Why it matters

Engineers are becoming reviewers and decision-makers for AI-driven design. Human-approval steps like Altium's show how to keep accountability.
