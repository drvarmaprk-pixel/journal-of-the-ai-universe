AI is tuning live mobile networks: T-Mobile says Ericsson AI-RAN features lifted beamforming and download speeds in New York at peak hours, and DOCOMO and NTT will demonstrate coordinated workload shifting and energy control at base stations.

Research, industry & policy

  • T-Mobile puts AI to work in its 5G Advanced network, T-Mobile · 20 Oct 2026: At peak hours the capability delivered about 50% more full-band SRS beamforming, 16% higher downlink user throughput, 12% better spectral efficiency and 10% more downlink data volume.
  • DOCOMO and NTT to begin world-first base-station workload and energy demo, NTT DOCOMO · 20 Oct 2026: The demonstration will coordinate shifting of computing workloads with energy management at radio base stations.

Journals & preprints

  • From Data to Deployment: A Scoping Review of Empirical Tools, Techniques, and Operational Readiness in Data-Driven 5G/6G Network Optimization, arXiv 2610.26566 · 19 Oct 2026: Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly applied to 5G/6G network optimization, yet differences in empirical evidence, evaluation methods, and computational requirements complicate comparisons of reported performance.
