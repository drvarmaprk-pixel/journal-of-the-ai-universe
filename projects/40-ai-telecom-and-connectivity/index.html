Daily AI News Updates on AI Telecom & Connectivity: project 40 of 50, in the AI Tech Stack group (36–41).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

AI is moving into the radio network. Nokia and SoftBank will run a commercial AI-RAN field trial in Japan in 2027, Ericsson says AI link adaptation runs in 60,000 base stations, and Samsung is starting industrial AI-RAN projects in Korea.

Top updates

  • Nokia and SoftBank plan commercial AI-RAN field trial, 6ix · 16 Oct 2026: Nokia anyRAN on SoftBank's AITRAS with NVIDIA ARC-Pro, in Japan in 2027.
  • Ericsson: AI-RAN value lies in ASICs, RCR Wireless · 16 Oct 2026: AI-native link adaptation in 60,000 base stations at three operators delivers about 10% spectral-efficiency gains.
  • Nokia sees 2x spectral efficiency by 2028, RCR Wireless · 2 Oct 2026: Nokia outlines a software path to 6G; Orange wants the gains proven in live networks.
  • Samsung's industrial AI-RAN projects, RCR Wireless · 2 Oct 2026: Projects with KT and SK Telecom under Korea's Hyper AI Network initiative.

Research

  • A Token Service Interface for AI-Native RANs, arXiv · 2 Oct 2026: Proposes an interface so radio networks can schedule AI token streams by purpose and timing.

Upcoming events

  • Brooklyn 6G Summit 2026, 3–5 Nov 2026, Brooklyn, NY: Nokia and NYU WIRELESS on AI-native 6G (invitation-only).
  • FOKUS FUSECO Forum 2026, 26–27 Nov 2026, Berlin: Fraunhofer FOKUS on next-generation networks.

Why it matters

Smarter radio networks squeeze more capacity from scarce spectrum and prepare the ground for AI-native 6G.
