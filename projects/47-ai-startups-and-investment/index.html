Latest updates · 18 October 2026

Big cheques keep flowing to AI. Modal raised $750M at a $15.75B valuation, Manus raised more than $500M after its Meta deal fell through, and Zenzap and Scimplify closed AI rounds.

Top updates

  • Modal raises $750M at $15.75B valuation, SiliconANGLE · 14 Oct 2026: Series D led by Accel; annualised revenue above $1bn.
  • Manus raises over $500M, TechCrunch · 8 Oct 2026: Led by Boyu and IDG — the first round since Meta's $2bn deal was called off.
  • Zenzap raises $44M, TechCrunch · 14 Oct 2026: Bessemer and Lightspeed back its AI workplace chat app.
  • Scimplify raises $25M, TechCrunch · 14 Oct 2026: Led by Hitachi Ventures, at about a $250M valuation, for AI-driven chemicals and critical materials.

Research

  • Is the business of frontier AI companies selling tokens?, arXiv · 9 Oct 2026: Reverse cash-flow maths suggests OpenAI and Anthropic together need $733bn–$2.79tn in year-ten revenue to justify valuations.

Upcoming events

  • TechCrunch Founder Summit 2026, 4 Nov 2026, Boston: A day for startup founders.
  • Slush 2026, 18–19 Nov 2026, Helsinki: The founder-and-investor gathering.

Why it matters

Funding shows where investors expect AI value to land — but valuations that need trillions in future revenue carry real risk. Not investment advice.
