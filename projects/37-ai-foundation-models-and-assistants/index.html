Daily AI News Updates on AI Foundation Models & Assistants: project 37 of 50, in the AI Tech Stack group (36–41).

Model launches are covered here.

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

Frontier and open models landed in the same week. Google launched Gemini 4 Argon, Meta released Muse Spark Open (816B) and Reflection AI released Beam (501B), while NVIDIA expanded open Nemotron.

Top updates

  • Introducing Gemini 4 Argon, Google · 13 Oct 2026: #1 on the Vals Index; $2/$10 per million input/output tokens until 30 Nov, then $4/$20; in the API, the Gemini app and AI Mode.
  • Meta releases Muse Spark Open, Meta AI · 15 Oct 2026: A multimodal open-weight model with 816B parameters (42B active) and a 1M-token context.
  • Reflection AI releases Beam, Hugging Face · 16 Oct 2026: A 501B open model 'you can own, customise and scale'.
  • NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, NVIDIA · 15 Oct 2026: An open 120B vision-language model; Nemotron Bolt 74B-A9B is available now and Bolt E4B follows on 23 Oct.

Research

  • LLM-jp-4 Technical Report, arXiv · 14 Oct 2026: Japanese sovereign 8B dense and 32B-A3B MoE models, trained from scratch on about 12 trillion tokens.
  • Large Language Continuous Diffusion Models, arXiv · 2 Oct 2026: Sigma, the first large-scale (3B/8B) continuous diffusion language model.

Upcoming events

  • GenAI London 2026, 20 Nov 2026, QEII Centre, London: Generative AI conference.
  • Generative AI Summit London, 1 Dec 2026, London: Builders and leaders on generative AI.

Why it matters

Open weights at frontier scale let anyone study and adapt top-tier models, while price cuts at the top make capable AI cheaper for everyone.
