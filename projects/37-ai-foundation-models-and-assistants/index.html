Nvidia has launched new open models for enterprise and on-device agents, and GoTo has released a 27-billion-parameter Gemma 3 model built with AI Singapore for Indonesian and four regional languages.

Research, industry & policy

  • Nvidia launches new open models to run agents, TechCrunch · 19 Oct 2026: The models target companies that want to run their own open models in their own data centres, and private agents on PCs.
  • GoTo releases a 27B open model for Indonesian languages, AI Lookout · 19 Oct 2026: The Gojek and Tokopedia group built the Gemma 3-based model with AI Singapore for Indonesian, Javanese, Sundanese, Balinese and Batak.

Journals & preprints

  • MIRA: A Multimodal Illustrated Deep Research Agent for Controllable Reports, arXiv 2610.25926 · 19 Oct 2026: Deep research agents are increasingly capable of searching the open web and synthesizing evidence, yet they remain poorly suited to controllable report generation in multimodal deep research.
  • Adaptation of Computer Use Agents to New Software with User Manuals, arXiv 2610.23012 · 16 Oct 2026: Recent advances in multimodal foundation models have significantly improved the ability to operate real-world software through graphical user interfaces (GUIs) using visual observations and natural language instructions.
