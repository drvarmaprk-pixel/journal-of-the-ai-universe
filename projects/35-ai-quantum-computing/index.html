AI agents are now designing quantum hardware. IBM's open agent factories optimised a QEC decoder chip design at a fraction of the cost of proprietary models.

Research, industry & policy

  • IBM: open agent factories for chip design, IBM Research · 13 Oct 2026: Open-model agents cut the estimated latency of a Relay-BP decoder design by 52x for about $26 in inference. Proprietary models reached 204x at about $299. These are HLS estimates, not fabricated chips.

Journals & preprints

  • Agentic AI for verifiable quantum compilation, arXiv 2610.23832 · 16 Oct 2026: Agents find more efficient circuit compilations when bottleneck subroutines can be checked classically.
