03 AI Defence

Daily AI News Updates on AI Defence: project 03 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 20 October 2026

In the full issue →

US Army Cyber Command says AI agents now staff a complete cyber protection team under daily human checks, the Navy is building standard command and control for drones, and India's Navy names AI, quantum and autonomy as priorities.

Research, industry & policy

  • Army Cyber Command builds an agentic cyber protection team, Federal News Network · 19 Oct 2026: Task Force Lexington has mapped every work role in a cyber protection team to AI agents, now handed to the joint force. Project Griffin will award two contracts for agents that respond to attacks autonomously.
  • Army Cyber Command on the human-AI balance, Defense News · 19 Oct 2026: Lt. Gen. Christopher Eubank said all agent work is still human-in-the-loop, with a governance board deciding what agents may do alone; one agent did in two days what took an analyst six months.
  • Indian Navy eyes AI, quantum and autonomous systems (India), CNBC-TV18 · 19 Oct 2026: Vice Chief Vice Admiral Ajay Kochhar said the Navy is developing AI, quantum and autonomous capabilities to become a multi-domain networked force.
  • Sea services weigh autonomous, attritable tech, National Defense · 19 Oct 2026: Adm. James Kilby told the National Commission on the Future of the Navy that common command and control and standard interfaces matter; the Navy set up a Robotic and Autonomous Systems Warfighting Development Center in late September.

Journals & preprints

  • Adaptive Cyber-Defense for Evolving Attacker Capabilities using Reinforcement Learning and Behavior Trees, arXiv 2610.25420 · 18 Oct 2026: Autonomous cyber-defense agents trained using reinforcement learning (RL) are increasingly adopted for defending enterprise computer networks.

Conferences, workshops & events

  • Delhi Defence Dialogue 2026, 3–4 Nov 2026 · New Delhi: Defence self-reliance, collaborations and exports; AI and quantum among emerging tech in focus.
  • MilCIS 2026, 17–19 Nov 2026 · Canberra: Military communications & information systems; agenda includes governing AI agents on defence networks.
  • DefenseTech Week 2026, 2–3 Dec 2026 · Tel Aviv University: Autonomous systems and AI-driven warfare, defence start-ups.

Why it matters

Militaries are moving from AI pilots to AI staff, so governance boards, audit trails and standards now matter as much as the models.
