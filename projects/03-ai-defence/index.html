03 AI Defence

Daily AI News Updates on AI Defence: project 03 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

The Pentagon now describes a four-star Autonomous Warfare Command as the end state of Project Agincourt, while AI command-and-control and AI drones move from trials to the field.

Top updates

  • Hegseth: Autonomous Warfare Command 'always the end state', US Department of War · 14 Oct 2026: US Secretary of War Hegseth calls Project Agincourt the pathway to a four-star Autonomous Warfare Command. Hundreds of thousands of one-way attack systems are to be fielded this year.
  • Anduril wins NGC2 contract, Defense News · 13 Oct 2026: The contract, worth up to $1.8bn, moves the US Army's AI-enabled Next Generation Command and Control (NGC2) from experiment to fielding.
  • Army to buy defensive AI agents, Breaking Defense · 13 Oct 2026: The US Army is preparing to award contracts for AI agents that defend its networks, which an official calls a 'cautious' first step.
  • AI drones in Russia and Ukraine, NPR · 12 Oct 2026: Both sides are now using AI-powered drones on the battlefield.

Research

  • The Ethics of AI in Military Operations, arXiv · 22 Sep 2026: A study of the ethical questions raised by using AI in military operations.
  • The Controllability Trap, arXiv · 3 Mar 2026: A governance framework for military AI agents. It is older but directly relevant to today's news.

Upcoming events

  • CAID – Conference on AI for Defence, 17–18 Nov 2026, Rennes, France: The 8th edition, held as part of European Cyber Week.
  • MilCIS 2026, 17–19 Nov 2026, Australia: A conference on military communications and information systems, with sessions on secure AI and AI agents.

Why it matters

As autonomy scales to hundreds of thousands of systems, questions of human control, accountability and escalation become urgent.
