The AI build-out keeps accelerating. Nebius became the first customer for VOLT's €7bn Rotterdam AI gigafactory, AMD backed Fermi's Texas campus, and Alphabet reportedly sees 2026 AI capex near $200bn.

Top updates

  • Nebius signs on to VOLT's €7bn Rotterdam AI gigafactory, Tech Times · 13 Oct 2026: Up to 100,000 GPU slots and 200 MW; VOLT also launched a Dutch AI Cloud.
  • Fermi and AMD sign credit support deal, DCD · 14 Oct 2026: Backing the first 222 MW at Project Matador near Amarillo, Texas, within a framework of up to 650 MW.
  • Alphabet's Porat pegs 2026 AI capex near $200B, AI Weekly · 14 Oct 2026: Above the $180–190bn guidance, with 2027 expected to 'increase significantly'. Editor's note: secondary summary of a Fortune report.

Research

  • An Optical KV Cache Network for Disaggregated LLM Serving, arXiv · 13 Oct 2026: Optical circuit switching to move KV caches between GPUs faster and cut time to first token.
