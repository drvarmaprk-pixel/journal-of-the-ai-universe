Daily AI News Updates on AI Robotics: project 11 of 50, in the Industries & Sectors group (01–14).

Latest updates · 18 October 2026

In the full issue →

Humanoids are learning from human data and heading for the factory floor. Galbot taught a Unitree G1 to navigate obstacles, Korea approved a 20-humanoid factory test, and Boston Dynamics is readying Atlas for production work.

Top updates

  • Galbot PASSAGE teaches humanoid navigation, Robot24 · 15 Oct 2026: The system used 100 hours of human motion data across 1,500 virtual environments to teach a Unitree G1 humanoid to navigate obstacles.
  • Korea clears 20-humanoid factory test, Seoul Economic Daily · 13 Oct 2026: It is Korea's first such test, with humanoids working alongside people at chip, car, shipbuilding and logistics sites.
  • Boston Dynamics: Atlas and Spot 5.2, TSN Media · 15 Oct 2026: Boston Dynamics is moving Atlas towards the factory floor, opening RMAC at Hyundai Metaplant and shipping new GR3 hands. Spot 5.2 adds Gemini-powered AIVI inspection.

Research

  • VioLA, arXiv · 8 Oct 2026: Learns general-purpose humanoid control policies from human data.
  • Being-M0.7, arXiv · 8 Oct 2026: A latent world-action model for humanoid robots.

Upcoming events

  • CoRL 2026 – Conference on Robot Learning, 9–12 Nov 2026 (workshops 12 Nov), Austin, Texas: A leading robot-learning conference.
  • IEEE-RAS Humanoids 2026, 7–9 Dec 2026, Santa Clara Convention Center, California: The 25th international conference on humanoid robots.

Why it matters

Learning from human motion and real factory trials are the two steps that turn humanoid demos into useful co-workers.
