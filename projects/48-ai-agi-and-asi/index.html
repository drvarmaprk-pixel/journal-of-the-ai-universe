Superintelligence has moved into policy documents: Nature examines its science, and the White House has issued an order on the 'era of super intelligence'. There are no verified AGI breakthrough claims. (A quiet period: items span 29 Sep–16 Oct.)

Top updates

  • The science of superintelligence, Nature · 7 Oct 2026: The article examines the science of superintelligence and argues that AI is still a useful word.
  • Anthropic's draft bill on catastrophic risk, Politico · 16 Oct 2026: The draft federal bill targets imminent catastrophic risks from frontier models, including loss of control.
  • A framework for frontier AI, Google DeepMind Institute · 30 Sep 2026: An essay setting out a framework for frontier AI and 'the dawning of a new age'.
  • 'Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence', The White House · 29 Sep 2026: A presidential action under that title.

Research

  • Measuring Progress Toward AGI: A Cognitive Framework, arXiv · May 2026 (older): A cognitive framework for measuring progress towards AGI.

Upcoming events
