Journals & preprints

  • ARCANA: A Benchmark for Abstraction and Analogical Reasoning, arXiv 2610.29271 · 21 Oct 2026: ARCANA asks solvers to map real objects and physical effects onto abstract grid puzzles; people do well, while language models fall clearly short.Evidence: Preprint (evidence step 4 of 6)Key finding: People reliably solve ARCANA's far-analogy puzzles, while a range of large language models show a clear performance gap.Significance: Technical: transferring ideas between distant domains remains a weak point for current models.Takeaway: A useful new yardstick for general reasoning; results are not yet peer-reviewed.New todayBenchmarks
