Research, industry & policy

  • Google and Amazon back nuclear plant upgrades, EEPower · 19 Oct 2026: Deals with Constellation Energy support nearly 1.1 GW of extra capacity in PJM through uprates, which raise output by upgrading turbines and generators.
  • Will electric companies protect consumer bills from data centres?, Union of Concerned Scientists · 19 Oct 2026: The analysis argues the rush to build data centres puts utilities and households in the middle of an affordability crisis.

Journals & preprints

  • Physics-Aware Distillation of a Grid Foundation Model for Real-Time Edge Inference, arXiv 2610.21833 · 15 Oct 2026: Grid foundation models (GridFMs) are graph transformers pretrained on a masked power-flow objective, and evaluate power flow orders of magnitude faster than an iterative solver.
  • Multi-site Learning for Forecasting Day-Ahead Wind-Power Production for Onshore Wind Farms in Great Britain with Deep Neural Networks, arXiv 2610.25370 · 18 Oct 2026: Accurate day-ahead wind-power production forecasting is increasingly important for energy system operations, market activity and balancing Great Britain's grid as wind generation grows.
