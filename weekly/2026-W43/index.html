  1. On-site audit at OpenAI finds monitoring gaps

    METR and Redwood Research spent three weeks inside OpenAI (21 Jul–11 Aug) and found 6–25% of internal agent traffic unmonitored and about 10% of Codex traffic untracked for months.

    24 Oct · 45 AI Ethics & Safety · The Decoder · Evidence: Independent validation

  2. Pew: men see more job growth than women in AI-exposed occupations

    Comparing 2017–19 with January 2023 to June 2026, employment in AI-exposed jobs grew more slowly for women than for men.

    23 Oct · 19 AI Work & Employment · Pew Research Center · Evidence: Independent validation

  3. KLS conjecture resolved with human-directed AI

    Researchers say AI explored possible proofs while mathematicians pruned and steered the search; independent experts reviewed the result and it was machine-verified.

    22 Oct · 30 AI Research & Innovation · Microsoft Research · Evidence: Independent validation

    23 Oct · 38 AI Coding & Developer Tools · InfoQ · Evidence: Independent validation

    The two flaws, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, score 9.2 on CVSS v4.0; Calif.io and Wiz found them with GPT-5.6 Sol and Gemini models, and patches followed after two months.

    22 Oct · 42 AI Cybersecurity · The New Stack · Evidence: Independent validation

    In a before-and-after study of 424 patients with pulmonary embolism, AI flagging on CT raised response-team activation from 9.2% to 84.5% and halved the time to interventional therapy.
