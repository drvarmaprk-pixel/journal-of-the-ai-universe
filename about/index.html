The project

Journal of the AI Universe (@RKVarma AI Universe) publishes Daily AI News Updates only: the latest ai news across every dimension of human civilisation.

Each issue covers 50 non-overlapping AI projects, numbered 01–50 and arranged in seven groups. Every project gets a short write-up of its top updates, research and upcoming events, with a link to every source, and a poster. Each issue is also published as a PDF journal.

Editorial policy

  • Only verified sources are used. Every link is checked, and each headline, date and figure in a write-up must match the source page.
  • Doubtful or unconfirmed secondary information is left out. Where a secondary summary is kept, or a source gives conflicting dates, it is flagged with an Editor’s note.
  • Every item links to its original source, so readers can check it themselves.
  • Each item belongs to exactly one project: for example, model launches go in 37, funding rounds in 47, data-centre deals in 39, and regulation and law in 43 or 06.
  • These are news summaries only, not medical, financial, legal or investment advice.
