Mission

Journal of the AI Universe is an independent, personal initiative by Dr.P.R.K.Varma, M.D., D.M., a cardiologist in India. It is part of RKVarma AI Universe, which tracks 50 AI projects.

The aim is to give readers a clear, sourced daily account of what is happening in artificial intelligence across every part of life. It has two daily publications: the Daily AI News Updates, which covers 50 AI projects numbered 01–50, and the Global AI Hardware & Software R&D Intelligence report, which follows the research of 50 companies.

Sourcing and verification

  • Primary sources first. Each item is based on the original source wherever one exists: the company or institution announcement, the paper or preprint, the official filing or the event page. Every item links to its sources so readers can check them.
  • Checked before publication. Headlines, dates, names and figures are checked against the source page. Anything that cannot be verified is dropped, not published with a caveat.
  • Secondary reports are flagged. Where only a secondary report is available and the item is still kept, or sources give conflicting details, this is stated with an Editor’s note.
  • Evidence labels on R&D items. Each item in the R&D Intelligence report is labelled with its evidence type (for example peer-reviewed paper, preprint, conference abstract, company announcement or secondary report) and its technology readiness level (from theoretical to commercial), so readers can see how strong the evidence is.
  • Evidence labels in the daily journal. From the 22 October 2026 issue, each news and research item carries a plain evidence label: Peer-reviewed, Preprint, Independent validation, Laboratory demonstration, Commercial deployment or Company announcement. A company announcement is reported as the company’s own claim and is never described as a breakthrough. The day’s top stories are ranked by scientific significance and strength of evidence, not by publicity, and clinical items give the study design, sample size, validation, comparator, outcomes and limitations where the source states them.
  • Daily reporting windows in IST. Each daily edition covers a stated reporting window in Indian Standard Time (IST). The R&D report prints its window at the top of every edition.
  • One home per item. In the Daily AI News Updates, each item is filed under exactly one of the 50 projects, so nothing is counted twice.
  • These are news and research summaries, not medical, financial, legal or investment advice.

Corrections

Mistakes are corrected openly. To report an error, post in the WhatsApp group, giving the date of the issue or report, the project or company number and what is wrong, with a link to a source if you have one.

When a correction is made, the item is amended and a dated Corrections note is added to that issue’s page, saying what was changed and when. Every correction and significant update is listed in the public Corrections & updates log. Factual errors are not changed silently. Typing and formatting fixes that do not change the meaning may be made without a note.

Independence and conflicts of interest

The journal is self-funded. It carries no advertising and no sponsored content, and nobody pays for an item to be included, left out or placed. Inclusion is an editorial decision based on news and research value.

If the editor has a personal or financial interest relevant to an item, it will be declared beside that item.

Use of AI tools

AI tools help to find and gather items, summarise sources, draft text and lay out the pages, posters and PDFs. Every item is curated and reviewed by the editor before publication, and every item links to its original sources. The editor is responsible for what is published.

Privacy

The site does not ask for, collect or store any personal information from visitors. It sets no cookies.

The site runs no statistics or analytics service and no advertising or tracking scripts.

There is no email newsletter yet, so the site has no forms and collects no email addresses.

Choices saved in your browser. Your theme (light or dark), the categories you follow and your answers to “Was this section useful?” are saved only in your own browser’s local storage and are never sent anywhere. You can clear them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Offline reading. The site can be installed as an app. A service worker keeps a copy of recently read pages on your device so they open without a connection; nothing is sent to anyone.

Search and sharing. Search runs entirely in your browser. Share buttons are plain links: nothing is loaded from WhatsApp, LinkedIn or X until you choose to share.

Services the site relies on. Like any web host, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in their logs for security. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives the same technical request data. These services handle that data under their own privacy policies.

External links. Links to sources and to the WhatsApp group take you to other sites, which have their own privacy policies. If you join the WhatsApp group, your phone number and profile are visible to the group under WhatsApp’s terms.

If anything here changes (for example, if an email newsletter or a statistics service is added), this policy will be updated at the same time.
